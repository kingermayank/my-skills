# Source notes and adaptation

## Source and review method

Adapted for the user's personal workflow from Anthony Wood / House of gAi, [April 26: AI for Branding Masterclass](https://courses.houseofgai.com/products/april-26-ai-for-branding-masterclass). Accessed through the user's signed-in course session on 19 November 2026. This is an independently written working synthesis, not an official House of gAi skill or a reproduction of the course.

The course inventory contained 62 lesson pages across two syllabus pages. Their lesson text was read. There were 43 embedded videos, including the two cohorts' live recordings and graduation presentations, totalling approximately 24 hours of source media.

All 43 video audio tracks were transcribed locally with whisper.cpp: base.en for instructional lessons and tiny.en for the longer live sessions. Review combined written lessons, transcript sections, and six distributed storyboard-frame samples per video. Long-session excerpts were selected across recording intervals for technical and branding discussion. This was **not continuous audiovisual playback or verification of every frame**. Machine transcripts contain transcription errors and repetitive hallucinations; named entities, tool settings, legal assertions, and quantitative claims were not accepted merely because they appeared in a transcript.

Seven distinct course PDFs were retrieved and their text extracted: client briefs, creative-director prompting, strategy, tone of voice, image prompting, motion, and the ethics/provenance checklist (54 pages in total). Their pages were rendered for visual inspection. The trusted-reference text file and three demo reference images were retrieved. The supplied strategy and final-case-study example archives were extracted; their PDF text was available, and representative presentation pages were visually sampled. Example student identities, personal stories, and artwork are not embedded in this skill.

The lesson-level ledger is [course-coverage.csv](course-coverage.csv). It records page URLs, video duration, audio transcription status, and the visual-review method. External inspiration sites and linked third-party case studies were not exhaustively crawled; optional community posts, forms, and live-room interactions were outside this synthesis.

## Deliberate adaptations

- Replaced platform-specific roles, subscription requirements, filenames, and chat rituals with optional portable project records and capability-based tool choice.
- Retained the durable four-part collaboration, seven-dimension image, and five-dimension motion frameworks; the wording and operational examples are newly written.
- Treated course parameters, model comparisons, subscription availability, regional legal summaries, and claims about interface quality as historical context requiring fresh verification when relevant.
- Did not preserve broad copyright/ownership guarantees based on a tool, nationality, prompting method, or amount of human editing. Provenance is evidence of process rather than legal clearance.
- Distinguished a vector-looking generation from an actual editable vector, exact colour values from generated swatch approximations, and “6-sheet” poster format from the separate six-image exercise.
- Added explicit records for assumptions, source dates, decision status, invariants, and unproduced assets to support handoff between different agents.
- Kept iteration and user judgement while removing classroom deadlines, certificates, referral links, community submissions, and automatic pauses unrelated to completing the user's work.

Raw course videos, transcripts, handouts, and example artworks remain outside the distributable skill folder. The package contains original condensed instructions and source references.
